About PDTG Transport Punjab

We publish practical transport guides and a public route-permit verification tool for Punjab.

Advertisement

What this site does

PDTG Transport Punjab helps citizens, buyers, and operators understand vehicle checks, token tax basics, smart-card tracking concepts, and route-permit records. Our verify tool looks up route permits from the PTA permit database.

What this site is not

We are not the official Excise & Taxation MTMIS portal and not a government office. For ownership registration and token-tax payments, always confirm on official channels such as mtmis.excise.punjab.gov.pk.

How we keep guides useful

Processes can change. We update pages when public procedures change and clearly separate our permit lookup from Excise services.
