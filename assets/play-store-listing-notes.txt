---

Vehicle Hub PK — One window for Excise guides. Just a click away.

Browse guides and links to Excise, traffic, and driving licence portals across Pakistan. Review the official source, then open the correct government website in your browser.

WHAT THIS APP HELPS YOU FIND
• Official vehicle verification websites by province
• Official token tax and payment portals
• Official e-smart card / tracking tools
• Official e-challan related government portals
• Official driving licence apply / renew / verify websites by region
• Province portals for Punjab, Sindh, KPK, Islamabad, Balochistan, and Gilgit

OFFICIAL SOURCES
• Punjab Excise — https://excise.punjab.gov.pk/
• Punjab Vehicle Verification (MTMIS) — https://mtmis.excise.punjab.gov.pk/
• Punjab DLIMS — https://dlims.punjab.gov.pk/
• Sindh Excise — https://excise.gos.pk/
• Sindh DLS — https://dls.gos.pk/
• Islamabad Excise — https://islamabadexcise.gov.pk/
• Islamabad Police DLIMS — https://dlims.islamabadpolice.gov.pk/
• KPK Dastak — https://dastak.kp.gov.pk/
• KPK Dastak Citizen (Licence) — https://dastakappecitizenkp.pk/
• Balochistan Excise — https://excise.balochistan.gov.pk/
• Quetta Traffic Police — https://qtp.gob.pk/
• Gilgit Excise — https://gbexcise.gov.pk/
• GB Police DLMIS — https://dlmis.gbp.gov.pk/

HOW THE APP WORKS
Home → Choose province / service → Information page (source + official URL + disclaimer) → Open Official Government Website → Chrome / browser.

Vehicle Hub PK only explains steps and opens the correct website. It does not provide government services itself.
