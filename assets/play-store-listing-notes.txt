FULL DESCRIPTION (append / replace disclaimer section)
------------------------------------------------------
Vehicle Hub PK — One window for Excise services. Just a click away.

Browse guides and links to Excise, traffic, and driving licence portals across Pakistan. Open the correct government website for your province in one tap.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Open vehicle verification portals by province
• Open token tax and payment links
• Open e-smart card / tracking tools
• Open e-challan related services
• Apply, renew, or verify a driving licence by region
• Open province portals for Punjab, Sindh, KPK, Islamabad, Balochistan, and Gilgit

IMPORTANT — INDEPENDENT APP
Vehicle Hub PK is an independent informational app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Government of Pakistan or any provincial Excise & Taxation Department. All verification, payments, licences and government services are provided only through the official government websites.

OFFICIAL SOURCES (examples)
• Punjab Excise / MTMIS: https://mtmis.excise.punjab.gov.pk/
• Punjab Excise department: https://excise.punjab.gov.pk/
• Sindh Excise: https://excise.gos.pk/
• KPK Dastak: https://dastak.kp.gov.pk/
• Islamabad Excise: https://islamabadexcise.gov.pk/
• Balochistan Excise: https://excise.balochistan.gov.pk/
• Gilgit Excise: https://gbexcise.gov.pk/
• Punjab DLIMS: https://dlims.punjab.gov.pk/
• Sindh DLS: https://dls.gos.pk/
• Islamabad ITP DLIMS: https://dlims.islamabadpolice.gov.pk/
• KPK Dastak Citizen: https://dastakappecitizenkp.pk/
• Quetta Traffic Police: https://qtp.gob.pk/
• GB Police DLMIS: https://dlmis.gbp.gov.pk/

Vehicle Hub PK only explains steps and opens the correct website. It does not provide government services itself.
