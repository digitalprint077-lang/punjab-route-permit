Department mandate (summary)

  • Control of narcotics / intoxicants and related Excise taxes
  • Assessment and collection of profession, property, and related taxes
  • Registration of motor vehicles and collection of road tax (with federal withholding where applicable)

Important note

Balochistan Excise records are separate from Punjab, Sindh, KPK, and Islamabad systems. Always use the Balochistan portal for vehicles registered in Balochistan. Some online tools may show temporary downtime — retry later on the official site.
