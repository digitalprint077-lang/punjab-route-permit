Driving licence portals

Independent directory of official traffic / police websites for driving-licence services. Licensing is run by provincial, ICT, or GB authorities — not Excise.

Independent notice

Vehicle Hub PK does not issue, renew, or verify licences. All licence services are performed only on the official government websites listed below.

How to use this guide

  1. Choose the region that issued the licence.
  2. Open the official website linked above.
  3. Complete apply, renew, or verify steps only on that government website.
