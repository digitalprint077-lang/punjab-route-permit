Official resources only

Islamabad Excise publicly warns that only these legal channels should be used:

  • Website: islamabadexcise.gov.pk
  • App: CITY ISLAMABAD

Avoid unofficial third-party sites that claim to provide Islamabad Excise records.

Important note

Islamabad (ICT) records are separate from Punjab MTMIS. A Punjab search will not reliably show an Islamabad-registered vehicle.
