KPK — Dastak / digital licensing services

The official Dastak portal for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa focuses on digital arms licensing workflows (apply, renew, pay, track). Use the official site for live applications and status.

Official KPK Dastak portal

Visit the government portal for digital licensing services.

Visit dastak.kp.gov.pk

Service categories (from official portal)

CategoryWhat it is forNotes
Digital arms licensing Apply for and manage arms licenses online Main Dastak service area
Renewals & conversions Renew licenses and related copy/conversion workflows Handled inside the upgraded digital system
Verification checks Uses NADRA, criminal database, and police station records Part of application processing
E-payment Secure digital payments (PAYMIR / e-banking) Pay through official channels only
E-tracking & alerts Track application progress and receive updates Reduces office follow-up visits
Card printing Centralized card printing with QR code After approval stages complete
Biometric step NADRA office biometric verification when required Portal helps locate nearest options

Important note

Dastak is a KPK Home Department / KPITB digital licensing initiative. It is not the same as Punjab MTMIS vehicle verification. For KPK vehicle registration questions, always confirm on the relevant official Excise / MVRS channels published by the KPK government.
