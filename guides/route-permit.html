Route Permits in Punjab — What Operators Should Know

A route permit authorizes commercial movement under transport rules. It is different from Excise registration and should be checked on its own.

Who usually needs a route permit

Goods and passenger commercial categories — trucks, trailers, tankers, pickups, and similar vehicles — often operate under route permit paperwork. Private cars and motorcycles typically do not appear in a route-permit database.

Fields you should read carefully

  • Owner name and CNIC/NTN
  • Registration number, engine number, chassis number
  • Route permit number and application number
  • Valid from / valid upto and counter-signature dates
  • Issuing district and listed route districts

How to verify online on this site

  1. Open the public verify page.
  2. Enter the registration number.
  3. Compare the result with the vehicle stampings and seller documents.
  4. Open the full permit page if you need the printable summary / QR view.
