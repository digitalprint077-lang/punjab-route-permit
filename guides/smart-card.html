E-Smart Card (Vehicle Registration Card) in Punjab

Punjab has moved many vehicle records from the old paper book toward a chip-based smart card with a QR code for roadside checks.

What the smart card is for

The card stores key registration identity for the vehicle. Traffic police can use the QR / digital record to confirm details quickly. After a new registration or ownership transfer, printing and delivery can take time.

Advertisement

How to track smart-card status

  1. Keep your application / chassis references from Excise.
  2. Use the official Excise / MTMIS tracking options published for smart-card status.
  3. Watch for statuses such as printing, dispatched, or ready for collection (wording varies by portal update).
  4. Do not pay agents who promise “instant cards” outside official channels.

Smart card vs route permit QR

An Excise smart-card QR points to registration identity. A PDTG route-permit QR opens the public permit summary page. They solve different problems — ownership/registration versus route authorization.

Tips while waiting for a card

  • Keep payment receipts and application numbers safe.
  • Verify the vehicle online while the physical card is printing.
  • If status stalls for too long, visit the relevant Excise office with original documents.
