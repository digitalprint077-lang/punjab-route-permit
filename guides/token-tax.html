Punjab Token Tax — Check and Pay Basics

Token tax is the recurring road-tax style charge linked to many registered vehicles in Punjab. Buyers should confirm payment status before finalizing a deal.

What token tax is

Token tax is paid according to vehicle class and government schedules. Motorcycles often have lifetime token treatment at registration, while many cars pay on a periodic schedule. Always confirm the current rule for the vehicle in front of you on official channels.

Advertisement

How people usually check status

  1. Run the registration number on the official MTMIS portal.
  2. Review the latest payment / token related fields on the result.
  3. If dues exist, pay through the official e-Pay Punjab portal.
  4. Keep payment proof with the vehicle file.

Payment timing tips

  • Early full-year payments may qualify for published rebates in some years.
  • Digital payment channels sometimes offer an extra discount when announced by the province.
  • Unpaid token tax can become the buyer’s problem after purchase.

FAQ

Who should pay outstanding token tax before sale?

Best practice is to clear dues before transfer so the buyer does not inherit surprises. Put the arrangement in writing if you agree otherwise.

Where do I get the latest rates?

Use official Excise / e-Pay announcements for the current year. Third-party sites can explain the process but rate tables can change.
