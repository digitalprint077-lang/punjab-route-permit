Checks that prevent bad deals

Before buying a used vehicle, confirm ownership on the official Excise MTMIS portal, match engine and chassis numbers on the vehicle, and clear token tax / e-challan questions early.

District coverage

Punjab vehicle checks are provincial. Plate city codes show where a vehicle was registered, but you do not need a separate Lahore-only or Multan-only website for these lookups.
