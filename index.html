Two checks that prevent bad deals

For private cars and bikes, start with the official Excise MTMIS portal for ownership and tax clues. For trucks, tankers, pickups, and other commercial units, also verify the route permit on this site and match engine/chassis numbers on the vehicle itself.

District coverage

Punjab vehicle and permit checks are provincial. Plate city codes show where a vehicle was registered, but you do not need a separate Lahore-only or Multan-only website for these lookups.
