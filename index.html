What is PDTG Transport Punjab?

An online route-permit verification guide and lookup for citizens, buyers, and transport operators across Punjab.

Before this kind of check was online, confirming a commercial vehicle’s route paperwork often meant a visit to the transport office, paper files, and long waiting times. Now you can open the record on your phone: enter the registration number and see whether a matching route permit exists, who is named on it, and whether the dates still look valid.

This portal focuses on route permits issued under the Punjab Provincial Transport Authority system. It is separate from Excise MTMIS, which covers vehicle ownership registration and token tax. For a full safety check before buying or transferring a commercial vehicle, many people use both.

Built for Punjab’s freight roads

Trucks, tankers, trailers, and pickups move goods across districts every day. A clear permit record helps checkpoints, buyers, and operators trust the paperwork in front of them.

How to verify a route permit

The whole check takes under a minute. No login is required for the public.

  1. Open Verify Permit Use the Verify button on this site. Anyone can search — no staff account needed.
  2. Enter the registration number Start with the format printed on the plate, for example LEB-1234 or TAK 325 PESHAWAR.
  3. Try format variations if needed If the first search returns nothing, retry with and without spaces or dashes. Plate storage formats vary.
  4. Match the result to the vehicle Compare owner name, engine number, chassis number, and validity dates with the physical vehicle and papers.
  5. Open the full permit when needed From the result you can open the public permit page for the complete document view and QR link.

What details does verification show?

A successful search returns the core fields from the route permit record — similar in spirit to an MTMIS-style check, but for PTA route permits.

  • Owner details Full name, CNIC/NTN, and owner address as stored on the permit. The seller’s identity documents should match these fields before money changes hands.
  • Vehicle details Registration number, vehicle type (truck, tanker, pickup, and more), engine number, and chassis number. Match engine and chassis on the metal, not only on photocopies.
  • Permit & application details Route permit number (P.CO.P.NO), application number, fitness certificate number, issuing district, and authority footer text.
  • Validity window Valid from / valid upto dates, counter-signature upto, fee, and PSID references where recorded — so you can see if the permit period still looks current.
  • District coverage Other districts listed on the permit for route alignment across Punjab.

Why verification matters before you move

A clean-looking file is not enough. Matching live permit data to the vehicle protects buyers, sellers, and operators from fake papers and expired route rights.

Risks a one-minute check can catch

Use verification before deposits, transfers, or long-route deployments.

  • Wrong owner on record — the person presenting papers may not match the permit name.
  • Engine or chassis mismatch — a red flag for tampering or swapped documents.
  • Expired validity — the route window may already have ended.
  • Photocopy-only deals — informal letters without a live record create transfer headaches later.
  • District confusion — confirm the issuing district and listed route districts before you rely on the permit.

Also check Excise records when buying

Route permit verification does not replace MTMIS. For ownership name, token tax status, and registration history, open the official Excise portal as well, then compare both sets of details to the vehicle.

Common problems and quick fixes

Most “not found” results are formatting or timing issues — not always a missing vehicle.

  • Permit not found Retry with/without dashes and spaces. Confirm the plate is complete, including city segment if printed.
  • Owner name looks outdated The database shows the last completed permit record. Informal sales may not update names until formal processing finishes.
  • New permit not appearing yet Freshly issued records can take time to appear. Check again later before assuming an error.
  • Details look wrong on your own vehicle Contact the issuing transport authority with original documents for correction. Public search cannot edit records.
  • Need ownership / tax data Use the official MTMIS Excise portal for registration ownership and token tax — that is a different system.

Frequently asked questions

Answers for first-time users and roadside checks.

Is public verification free?

Yes. Anyone can check a registration number on this site at no cost. Staff login is only for issuing and managing permits.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the verify page, enter the registration number, and view matching permit records. Staff accounts are separate.

Is this the same as MTMIS Punjab?

No. MTMIS is the Excise vehicle registration and tax system. This portal checks Punjab route permit records from the PTA permit database. Use both when you need full confidence before a purchase or transfer.

What if no permit is found?

Try another plate format (spaces or dashes). The vehicle may not have a route permit in this database, or the record may still be processing. You can also open the official MTMIS portal for Excise ownership details.

Can I verify with a QR code?

Yes. If you have a permit QR, scan it to open the public permit page directly. You can also search by registration number on the verify page.

Can I check a motorcycle or private car here?

This lookup is built around route-permit records, which are typically for commercial/goods vehicles. Private cars and motorcycles are usually checked on Excise MTMIS for ownership and tax status.

Does one search cover all Punjab districts?

Yes. You do not need a separate Lahore or Multan portal for this permit database. District names on the permit show registration/route alignment, not a different website.

What should I check before buying a used commercial vehicle?

Verify the route permit here, verify ownership/tax on official MTMIS, match engine and chassis physically, confirm the seller’s CNIC against the owner fields, and insist on proper transfer paperwork.
