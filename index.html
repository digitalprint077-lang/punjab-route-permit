What is this portal?

PDTG Transport Punjab helps citizens and transport operators confirm route permit records issued under the Punjab Provincial Transport Authority.

Enter a vehicle registration number and the system looks up matching permit data — owner name, CNIC, vehicle type, engine and chassis numbers, permit number, and validity dates — so you can check paperwork before a deal, a checkpoint, or a transfer.

This site verifies route permits from the PTA permit database. For Excise ownership and token tax records, use the official MTMIS portal separately.

How to verify a route permit

The whole check takes under a minute. No login is required for the public.

  1. Open the verify page Go to Verify Permit or open verify.html on this site.
  2. Enter the registration number Use the format on the number plate first, for example LEB-1234 or TAK 325 PESHAWAR.
  3. Press Verify If nothing appears, try with and without spaces or dashes — plate formats vary.
  4. Match the result to the vehicle Compare owner name, engine, and chassis with the physical vehicle and documents.

What details does verification show?

A successful search returns the core fields from the route permit record.

  • Owner details Full name, CNIC/NTN, and address as stored on the permit.
  • Vehicle details Registration number, vehicle type, engine number, and chassis number.
  • Permit details Route permit number, application number, issuing district, and validity dates.
  • Public permit view Open the full permit page from the result when you need the complete document layout.

Why verify before you move

A one-minute check can stop costly mistakes on commercial vehicles and route paperwork.

  • Confirm the registered owner matches the person presenting the papers.
  • Match engine and chassis numbers against the vehicle itself.
  • See whether the route permit is still within its valid dates.
  • Avoid relying only on photocopies or informal transfer letters.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers for first-time users.

Is public verification free?

Yes. Anyone can check a registration number on this site at no cost. Staff login is only for issuing and managing permits.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the verify page, enter the registration number, and view matching permit records. Staff accounts are separate.

Is this the same as MTMIS Punjab?

No. MTMIS is the Excise vehicle registration and tax system. This portal checks Punjab route permit records from the PTA permit database. Use both when you need full confidence before a purchase or transfer.

What if no permit is found?

Try another plate format (spaces or dashes). The vehicle may not have a route permit in this database, or the record may still be processing. You can also open the official MTMIS portal for Excise ownership details.

Can I verify with a QR code?

Yes. If you have a permit QR, scan it to open the public permit page directly. You can also search by registration number on the verify page.
