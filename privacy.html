Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PDTG Transport Punjab handles information when you use our website.

Who we are

PDTG Transport Punjab provides public information and route-permit verification tools for visitors in Pakistan. We are not the official Excise MTMIS portal.

Information we collect

  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device, and pages visited (via hosting/analytics if enabled).
  • Registration numbers you voluntarily enter on the verify page to look up permit records.
  • Messages you send through the contact page.

Cookies and advertising

We may show third-party ads (for example Google AdSense). Ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads and measure performance. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Personalized ads can often be managed through your Google Ads Settings.

Third-party links

Guides on this site may link to official government portals such as mtmis.excise.punjab.gov.pk. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Data storage

Permit records shown through verification come from our connected permit database. We do not sell personal contact lists.

Children

This site is intended for general public and transport use. It is not directed at children under 13.

Updates

We may update this policy as advertising or hosting tools change. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use our Contact page.
